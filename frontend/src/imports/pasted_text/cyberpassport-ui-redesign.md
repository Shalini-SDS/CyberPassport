# CyberPassport – Complete UI/UX Redesign

Design a premium AI-powered cybersecurity identity platform called **CyberPassport**.

The platform should feel like a combination of:

* Digital Passport
* Credit Score Dashboard
* LinkedIn Profile
* Government Identity Credential

Avoid making it look like a SOC dashboard, hacker terminal, SIEM platform, or cybersecurity monitoring console.

The experience should focus on the user's digital identity, trust score, security reputation, and personal cybersecurity improvement journey.

---
